Business model

CustomersShoppers who want honest reviews; small sellers who want customer feedback.
ValueOne ranked score from ratings, personalised by the user’s own priorities, instead of a single star average.
RevenueFree for shoppers. Sellers pay for feedback analytics and verified-review badges; premium comparison reports.
CostHosting, moderation of fake reviews, development.
